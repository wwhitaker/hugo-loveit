KumaStatusBar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

KumaStatusBar doesn't collect any data. Nothing you enter or that the app shows is sent to the developer or anyone else. Everything stays on your Mac.

What the app stores

To show your status page, KumaStatusBar saves the settings you enter, on your Mac only:

  • Your Uptime Kuma home and remote URLs, status page slug, check interval and alert sound choices, in the app's own settings.
  • If you enter one, your Cloudflare Access service token (Client ID and secret), in your Mac's Keychain.

Deleting the app removes its settings. You can remove the token at any time in Settings → Connection.

Who the app talks to

KumaStatusBar only connects to the Uptime Kuma URLs you enter, to read your public status page. The Cloudflare Access token is only sent to your remote URL, never to your home URL. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and no servers of its own.

Notifications and permissions

Notifications and the local network permission are used only to alert you about your monitors and to reach your Uptime Kuma server on your home network. Both are handled by macOS on your Mac.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
